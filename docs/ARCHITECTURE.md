# POG Architecture

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Adopted
**Published:** 2026-08-02

---

# Overview

Property Operations Guide (POG) is a standards-based operational framework for homeowners and small property owners.

Rather than being a collection of random documents, POG organizes every aspect of property ownership into repeatable, documented operational processes.

POG is designed to evolve over time while maintaining consistency through documentation standards, decision records, templates, and version control.

---

# Design Goals

The framework is intended to be:

- Repeatable
- Maintainable
- Scalable
- Secure
- Vendor Neutral
- Technology Agnostic
- Easy for homeowners to adopt

---

# Architectural Layers

## Layer 1 — Foundation

Defines the guiding principles of POG.

Examples:

- Foundation
- Architecture
- Data Classification
- Governance

---

## Layer 2 — Standards

Defines *how* activities are performed.

Examples:

- Inspection Standards
- Maintenance Standards
- Vendor Standards
- Documentation Standards
- Naming Standards

---

## Layer 3 — Templates

Reusable documents containing placeholders but no private information.

Examples:

- Inspection Forms
- Tenant Tour Scorecards
- Maintenance Logs
- Vendor Evaluation Forms
- Property Inventory Forms

---

## Layer 4 — Operations

Property-specific operational documentation.

Examples:

- Maintenance History
- Inspection Reports
- Improvement Projects
- Vendor Records

These records belong in the future Private POG repository.

---

## Layer 5 — Secure Records

Highly confidential records maintained outside the public repository.

Examples:

- Financial Documents
- Leases
- Insurance Policies
- Access Credentials
- Legal Documents

---

# Supporting Documentation

The framework also includes:

- Decision Records (ADR)
- Decision Log
- Idea Log
- Project Roadmap
- Change History

These documents provide continuity as POG evolves.

---

# Guiding Principle

POG should make owning and operating residential property predictable, repeatable, and well documented.

Every document should support that objective.
