# POG Idea Log

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Published:** 2026-08-02

---

# Purpose

The Idea Log captures thoughts, enhancements, new projects, process improvements, and future capabilities before they are forgotten.

Ideas are intentionally captured without requiring immediate discussion or implementation.

The goal is to preserve creativity while allowing current work to continue uninterrupted.

---

# Workflow

Whenever an idea is identified during discussion, it should be recorded using the standard POG Idea format.

Unless otherwise requested, work should immediately return to the previous topic.

# Standard Idea Template

## Idea ID

IDEA-YYYYMMDD-###
(Example: IDEA-20260802-001)

---

### Recorded

Date and time the idea was captured.

---

### Category

Feature, Enhancement, Research, etc.

---

### Priority

Default: Untriaged

Possible values:

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low
- Untriaged

---

### Status

Possible values:

- Idea
- Under Review
- Planned
- In Progress
- Completed
- Deferred
- Rejected

---

### Title

Short descriptive title.

---

### Description

A concise description of the idea.

---

### Notes

Optional implementation notes, references, or links.

Every good idea deserves to be captured.

Not every idea deserves immediate implementation.
