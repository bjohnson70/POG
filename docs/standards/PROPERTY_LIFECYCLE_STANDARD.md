# Property Lifecycle Standard

**Document ID:** STD-LIFE-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Adopted  
**Published:** 2026-08-02

---

# Purpose

This standard defines the lifecycle of a residential property managed under the Property Operations Guide (POG).

Understanding the lifecycle helps owners plan maintenance, renovations, budgeting, tenant turnover, and long-term capital improvements.

---

# Objectives

The property lifecycle should:

- Organize operational activities
- Support long-term planning
- Improve budgeting
- Reduce deferred maintenance
- Preserve property value
- Capture institutional knowledge

---

# Property Lifecycle Phases

## Phase 1 — Acquisition

Activities include:

- Due diligence
- Inspections
- Property profile creation
- Initial inventory
- Baseline photographs
- Establish Property ID

Deliverables:

- Property Profile
- Initial Inspection
- Deferred Maintenance List

---

## Phase 2 — Preparation

Activities include:

- Repairs
- Cleaning
- Safety improvements
- Vendor onboarding
- Documentation

Deliverables:

- Ready-for-Occupancy Checklist
- Maintenance Completion Records

---

## Phase 3 — Active Operations

Activities include:

- Routine inspections
- Preventive maintenance
- Corrective maintenance
- Vendor management
- Budget tracking
- Tenant support (if applicable)

Deliverables:

- Inspection Reports
- Maintenance Logs
- Vendor Performance Records

---

## Phase 4 — Improvement

Activities include:

- Renovations
- Capital improvements
- Equipment replacement
- Energy efficiency upgrades

Deliverables:

- Project Records
- Warranty Documentation
- Updated Property Inventory

---

## Phase 5 — Turnover

Examples:

- Tenant move-out
- Seasonal occupancy change
- Ownership transition

Activities include:

- Inspection
- Cleaning
- Repairs
- Documentation updates

Deliverables:

- Turnover Inspection
- Move-In / Move-Out Records

---

## Phase 6 — Retirement

Examples:

- Sale
- Demolition
- Conversion
- Removal from POG

Activities include:

- Archive records
- Final inspection
- Retain historical documentation

---

# Continuous Activities

The following occur throughout the lifecycle:

- Documentation
- Inspections
- Maintenance
- Vendor Management
- Budget Planning
- Risk Management
- Idea Capture
- Decision Tracking

---

# Success Metrics

A well-managed property should demonstrate:

- Reduced emergency repairs
- Accurate maintenance history
- Reliable vendor relationships
- Predictable operating costs
- High documentation quality
- Preserved property value
