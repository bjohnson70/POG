# Vendor Management Standard

**Document ID:** STD-VEND-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Adopted
**Published:** 2026-08-02

---

# Purpose

This standard establishes the process for selecting, evaluating, documenting, and managing vendors performing work on properties managed under the Property Operations Guide (POG).

Effective vendor management improves workmanship, reduces costs, preserves property value, and creates long-term relationships with trusted service providers.

---

# Objectives

Vendor management should be:

- Consistent
- Objective
- Well documented
- Performance based
- Relationship oriented
- Risk aware

---

- Electrician

Additional categories may be added as needed.

---

# Vendor Identification

Each vendor receives a permanent identifier.

Example:

```
VEN-0001
VEN-0002
VEN-0003
```

Vendor IDs are never reused.

---

# Vendor Profile

Each vendor should maintain:

- Vendor ID
- Business Name
- Primary Contact
- Phone
- Email
- Website
- Service Area
- Trade Category
- License Number (if applicable)
- Insurance Verified
- Preferred Vendor Status
- Notes

Private contact information belongs in the future Private Operations Repository.

---

# Vendor Evaluation Criteria

Evaluate vendors using the following criteria:

- Communication
- Professionalism
- Quality of Work
- Timeliness
- Reliability
- Cost Competitiveness
- Cleanliness
- Warranty Support
- Documentation
- Overall Satisfaction

---

# Preferred Vendor Program

Preferred vendors consistently demonstrate:

- High quality workmanship
- Reliable scheduling
- Fair pricing
- Professional communication
- Proper licensing
- Appropriate insurance
- Strong customer service

Preferred status should be reviewed periodically.

---

# Work Orders

Every significant vendor engagement should document:

- Property ID
- Vendor ID
- Date
- Work Performed
- Cost
- Warranty
- Before Photos (optional)
- After Photos (optional)
- Lessons Learned

---

# Vendor Performance Review

Vendor performance should be reviewed after major projects and periodically for recurring service providers.

Repeated poor performance should result in removal from the Preferred Vendor list.

# Guiding Principle

The cheapest vendor is rarely the least expensive over the life of a property.

POG values reliability, quality, communication, and long-term relationships over lowest initial cost.
